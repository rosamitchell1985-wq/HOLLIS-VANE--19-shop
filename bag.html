What happens after you order

  • Stocked pieces leave our Secaucus warehouse the same business day if ordered before 14:00 ET.
  • Free US ground shipping on orders of $250 or more, flat $9 below that. Full rates.
  • Thirty days to return or exchange from delivery. Exchanges are free both ways. The process.
  • Made-to-order pieces are cut after you order, with the lead time stated on the product page before you pay.
  • Repairs are free for the life of the garment, with no receipt and no time limit.

Not sure about a size?

Do not guess. Send us the flat measurements of a garment you already own and like, tell us which piece you are considering, and we will answer with a recommendation and the numbers behind it.
