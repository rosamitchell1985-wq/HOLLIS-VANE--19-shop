Visiting our own studio

If you are in New York, the studio at 214 Lafayette Street, Suite 4 is open by appointment on Thursdays and Fridays, 11:00 to 17:00. Every piece in every size is on the rail, there is a proper mirror and there is no obligation to buy anything.

Book by writing to clientcare@hollisandvane.com or calling +1 (212) 555-0148. Appointments run 45 minutes and we hold two at a time, so the room is never crowded.

Wholesale enquiries

We work with a small number of retail partners and we are open to more, particularly in the Mountain West and the Southwest where we currently have one store between us.

Terms, stated up front

  • Minimum opening order of $6,000 at wholesale, reorders from $2,000.
  • Wholesale is 50% of our published retail price, with a recommended retail price that matches ours.
  • Payment in advance on the first order, net 30 thereafter.
  • No exclusive territories. We will tell you honestly who else is nearby before you commit.
  • No minimum advertised price policy beyond a request that you do not discount within the first season.
  • Free replacement of faulty stock, and the same free repair service your customers would get from us directly.
  • We supply photography, measurements and care copy at no charge for your own site.

We do not sell to marketplaces, dropshippers or resellers without a physical store or an established editorial site. Wholesale is about 14% of our revenue and we would like it to stay roughly there.
