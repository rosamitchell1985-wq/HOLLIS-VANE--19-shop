Capsule 08  /  Red Hook, Brooklyn, 4 and 5 February 2026

Campaign

Capsule 08

Shot over two cold mornings on Van Brunt Street with two models, one camera and no stylist. Everything photographed here is in the collection at the price listed on its product page. Nothing was pinned, clipped or taped to fit.

Advertisement

Ad unit 728x90 / responsive

How the campaign was made

We photograph our own collection because hiring a production company costs more than the cloth for a full run of coats. Two mornings, a borrowed Leica, one roll of 35mm per look, and a friend who has shot for us since 2019.

The models are Nadia Okonkwo and Emil Rasmussen, both booked through a New York agency and both paid at standard day rates. Their heights and the sizes they wear are listed under every frame, on every product page, and in the size guide, because a photograph without that information tells you almost nothing about fit.

No garment was pinned or clipped. If a coat looks slightly large on a frame, that is how it fits that person at that size.

Buying what you see

Every look above is assembled from pieces that stay in stock year round, with two exceptions: The Shearling Car Coat and The Weekend Holdall are cut to order.

  • Outerwear, eight styles from $395
  • Knitwear, eight styles from $190
  • Accessories, seven styles from $145
  • New Arrivals, the five most recent additions

If you want to know what a piece looks like on a body closer to your own measurements, write to client care. We keep additional fit photographs on file at several sizes and we will send them.
