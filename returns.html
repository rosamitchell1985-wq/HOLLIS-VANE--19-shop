After the order

Returns and exchanges

Thirty days from delivery, on anything unworn with the tags attached. Exchanges cost you nothing in either direction. Refund returns carry a flat $7 label fee. If something is faulty or we sent the wrong piece, you pay nothing at all.

Who pays what

  • Exchange, any reason. We pay the return label and the outbound shipping on the replacement. Cost to you: nothing.
  • Refund, changed your mind. We send a prepaid label and deduct a flat $7 from the refund. There is no restocking fee and no percentage deduction.
  • Faulty, damaged or wrong item sent. We pay everything, including the return label and the replacement. Nothing is deducted.
  • Your own carrier. You are welcome to return at your own cost with any carrier. In that case nothing is deducted from the refund, but we cannot be responsible for a parcel that does not arrive, so use tracking.

The process, step by step

  1. Email returns@hollisandvane.com or use the contact form with your order number and what you would like to do.
  2. We reply within one business day with a prepaid label and, for exchanges, we place a seven-day hold on the replacement size so it cannot sell out while your parcel is in transit.
  3. Repack the piece in any box, attach the label and drop it at any carrier point. Keep the receipt until the refund lands.
  4. We inspect on arrival, usually within one business day of delivery to Secaucus.
  5. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within three business days of inspection. Your bank then takes a further three to five days to show it.
  6. Exchanges ship the same day we inspect, with tracking emailed as usual.

Condition we expect

Unworn, unwashed, unaltered, with the tags still attached and free of smoke, perfume and pet hair. Trying something on indoors is exactly what the thirty days are for. Wearing a coat to a wedding and returning it on the Monday is not, and we will decline it and send it back at our cost with a note explaining why.

Knitwear returned with visible wear at the cuff or elbow, and leather goods returned with scratches, cannot be resold as new and will be declined.

Exceptions

  • Made-to-order pieces can be refunded within 30 days but cannot be exchanged for a different size or specification, because there is no second one waiting. This covers The Shearling Car Coat, The Weekend Holdall, cut-to-waist belts and monogrammed card cases.
  • Free alterations. A hemmed trouser or shortened sleeve remains fully refundable, since the alteration is ours to reverse.
  • Gift cards cannot be returned or exchanged for cash, except where state law requires it.
  • Seconds and sample sale pieces are sold with the fault described and are final sale. This is stated at the point of purchase, not buried here.

Gifts

If you received something as a gift, write to client care with the order number from the card or the name of the person who bought it. We issue a gift card for the value rather than refunding the purchaser, and we do not tell them unless you ask us to.

International returns

Customers in Canada, the UK and the EU have the same 30 days. Because we cannot issue prepaid labels across borders reliably, international returns are sent at your own cost with a carrier of your choice, and we refund the goods in full with nothing deducted.

Mark the parcel clearly as a returned good on the customs declaration and include the original invoice. If you do not, we may be charged import duty to receive your own return, and we will have to pass that on.

Duty and VAT you paid on the original delivery is usually reclaimable from your own customs authority rather than from us. The carrier can normally tell you how.

Repairs, which are not returns

Anything that fails after the return window is a repair, and repairs are free for the life of the garment. Reknitting, reweaving, rebuttoning and hemming cost nothing. Leather restitching and zip replacement are charged at cost, because that work happens at our workshop in Spain.

Send the piece to the studio address in the footer with a note, or start the process through client care. We confirm receipt within two business days and give you a completion date within five.

Your statutory rights

Nothing on this page limits your rights under applicable United States federal or state consumer protection law, or under the consumer law of your own country if you are ordering from outside the US. Where those rights are stronger than the terms above, those rights apply.
