The Leather Tote

$690

One rectangle of vegetable-tanned cowhide, folded and closed at two seams. No lining, no zip, no plate, no logo. The structure comes entirely from the thickness of the hide, which is 2.2 mm at the base and slightly thinner at the handles.

It stands when empty and slouches as it fills. Vegetable-tanned leather darkens where your hand goes and where the sun reaches, so within six months the bag records how you carry it. That is either the appeal or the problem, and we would rather you decide before you buy.

Size  /  Size Guide

Fabric

  • Vegetable-tanned cowhide, 2.2 mm, from a Tuscan tannery in the Consorzio Vera Pelle
  • Tanned with tree bark over roughly forty days, no chromium salts
  • Hide is a byproduct of the food industry and traceable to farm
  • Unlined, with edges burnished by hand and sealed with beeswax
  • Saddle stitched at the base in waxed linen thread, two needles, by hand
  • No hardware of any kind

Fit

  • 15 in wide by 13 in high by 5 in deep
  • Fits a 15 in laptop flat against the back panel
  • Handle drop of 9 in clears the shoulder over a winter coat
  • Holds roughly 17 litres
  • Weight empty: 1.7 lb

Care

  • Wipe with a dry cloth. Do not use saddle soap or conditioner in the first year while the leather is still taking colour
  • Expect uneven darkening from sun and hand oils. This is the material behaving correctly
  • Keep out of prolonged rain; vegetable-tanned leather spots permanently when soaked
  • Store loosely stuffed with paper, out of direct sun, never in plastic
  • Restitching and edge refinishing at our New York studio at cost, for the life of the bag

Sizing and model

  • One size
  • Photographed on a model of 5 ft 9 in for scale
  • No sizing decision is required
  • If you need a larger capacity, The Weekend Holdall is 38 litres and made to order
  • Dimensions are confirmed in the Size Guide

Shipping and returns

  • In stock and ships from Secaucus, New Jersey within one business day
  • Free US ground shipping on this item, since it is over the $250 threshold
  • 30 days to return or exchange from delivery, unused and unmarked
  • Free return shipping on exchanges, flat $7 label fee on refund returns
  • Restitching at cost for life

Vegetable tanning, and the trade it asks of you

Most leather in the world is chrome tanned. It takes about two days, produces a stable, uniform, water-resistant hide, and it is why a chrome-tanned bag looks the same on day one and day one thousand.

Vegetable tanning uses tannins from chestnut and mimosa bark and takes our tannery around forty days in pits. The hide comes out warmer in tone, firmer in hand, and alive to light and oil.

The trade is real. This bag will scratch, and the first scratch will show. Soak it and it will spot. In exchange you get a material that develops depth instead of wearing thin, and that a leatherworker can restitch indefinitely because nothing is glued.

If that trade does not suit how you live, The Structured Top-Handle is lined in cotton canvas and far more forgiving. We would rather sell you the right one once.
