The Mono Overcoat

$1,290

The coat the rest of the collection is built around. Cut from a double-face wool and alpaca woven by Lanificio Fratelli Piacenza in Biella, and made in an atelier outside Porto that has been finishing double-face cloth for three generations.

There is no lining, no fusible interlining and no shoulder pad. The two faces of the cloth are separated by hand at every edge and closed with a slip stitch, which is why it drapes rather than hangs and why it looks the same inside as out.

Size  /  Size Guide

Fabric

  • 80% virgin wool, 20% alpaca, double-face construction
  • 780 g per linear metre, 3.4 metres per coat
  • Woven by Lanificio Fratelli Piacenza, Biella, Italy
  • Every edge hand-separated and slip stitched, no bound seams
  • Two horn buttons from a British button house, a byproduct of the food industry
  • No lining, no fusible interlining, no shoulder pad

Fit

  • Relaxed straight body with a deliberate drop shoulder
  • Falls to mid-calf: 48 in centre back on size M
  • Room to layer a heavy knit underneath without pulling at the chest button
  • Sleeve cut long and straight, intended to sit at the base of the thumb
  • Between sizes, take the smaller one; the drop shoulder already adds width

Care

  • Dry clean sparingly, once or twice a season at most
  • Brush along the nap with a soft garment brush after each wear
  • Hang on a wide wooden hanger; wire hangers deform the shoulder within a season
  • Store with cedar, never in a sealed plastic garment bag
  • Free reweaving of moth damage and snags at our New York studio, for the life of the coat

Sizing and model

  • XS to XXL, equivalent to US 0 through US 18
  • First model is 5 ft 11 in with a 34 in chest and wears a size S
  • Second model is 6 ft 2 in with a 42 in chest and wears a size L
  • The coat is cut on one block for all bodies, not split by gender
  • If your chest measures within 2 in of a size boundary, size down

Shipping and returns

  • In stock and ships from Secaucus, New Jersey within one business day
  • Free US ground shipping on this item, since it is over the $250 threshold
  • 30 days to return or exchange from delivery
  • Free return shipping on exchanges, flat $7 label fee on refund returns
  • Repairs at cost for the life of the garment

Garment measurements in inches, taken flat and doubled where relevant. Chest is measured one inch below the armhole and doubled. Centre back length is taken from the base of the collar seam.

Where this coat comes from

Piacenza has woven cloth in Biella since 1733. We buy from them because double-face is one of the few constructions that cannot be faked: two webs are woven and bonded so that both sides are finished, and a mill either has the loom time and the skill or it does not.

The cloth arrives in Porto in rolls of 60 metres. It is rested for 48 hours before cutting so the fibres relax, which is the difference between a coat that hangs straight in year five and one that twists.

Finishing is the expensive part. A finisher separates the two faces at the hem, the front edge, the collar and every seam allowance, then closes them by hand. On a size M that is about 11 feet of slip stitching. One person can finish three coats in a working day if nothing goes wrong.

Landed cost, including cloth, making, trims, freight and duty, is a little over $430. We sell at $1,290. A department store carrying the same coat through wholesale and retail margin would need roughly $2,400 to make the same money.
