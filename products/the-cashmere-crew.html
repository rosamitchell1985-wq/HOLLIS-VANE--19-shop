The Cashmere Crew

$420

Two-ply grade A Inner Mongolian cashmere, knitted at a 7 gauge in Dumfries. That is heavier than most cashmere sold near this price, and the reason it holds a shape across the shoulder instead of collapsing after a winter.

Fully fashioned, meaning each panel is knitted to its final shape on the machine rather than cut from a knitted sheet. Nothing is cut, so nothing can unravel at the seam. The shoulders are linked by hand, stitch to stitch.

Size  /  Size Guide

Fabric

  • 100% grade A Inner Mongolian cashmere, 2 ply, 7 gauge
  • Fibre length 36 mm, diameter 15.5 microns
  • Spun in Todmorden, England; knitted in Dumfries, Scotland
  • Fully fashioned panels, hand-linked at the shoulder
  • Washed and finished in soft Scottish water, which is what gives the surface its bloom
  • Weight: 11.6 oz on a size M

Fit

  • Relaxed crew neck with a slightly dropped shoulder
  • Body length: 26 in on size M
  • Ribbed cuff and hem, 2.5 in deep, hold their shape after washing
  • Neck opening is cut to sit clear of a shirt collar
  • Take your usual size for a relaxed fall, one down for a close fit

Care

  • Hand wash cool in cashmere shampoo, or use a dedicated wool cycle at 30 degrees Celsius
  • Dry flat and reshape while damp; never hang wet cashmere
  • Expect light pilling in the first three wears, then it settles. Comb it out with a cashmere comb
  • Store folded with cedar, never on a hanger
  • Free reknitting of moth holes at our New York studio, for the life of the sweater

Sizing and model

  • XS to XXL, equivalent to US 0 through US 18
  • Model is 5 ft 9 in with a 36 in chest and wears a size M
  • Cut on one block for all bodies, not split by gender
  • If you want the sleeve to cover the hand, size up one
  • Cuff and hem stretch roughly 3 in without deforming

Shipping and returns

  • In stock and ships from Secaucus, New Jersey within one business day
  • Free US ground shipping on this item, since it is over the $250 threshold
  • 30 days to return or exchange from delivery
  • Free return shipping on exchanges, flat $7 label fee on refund returns
  • Free reknitting for life, at our studio

What you are paying for

Cashmere price is set by three things: fibre length, fibre diameter and ply. Ours is 36 mm long and 15.5 microns thick, spun into two plies. Cashmere sold at $99 is usually a single ply spun from fibre under 30 mm, which is why it pills into felt by February.

The yarn alone costs us $77 per sweater. Knitting, hand-linking and finishing in Dumfries add roughly $84.

We do not dye this sweater to a fashion colour. It exists in one black and one warm grey, because a dyehouse minimum is 300 kg and every additional colour is inventory we would eventually have to discount. Not discounting is the whole business model.

If the sweater fails, we reknit it. In 2025 we repaired 214 garments, 61 of them cashmere crews, most of them moth damage from summer storage. That service is free and it is not time limited. More on that in Sustainability & Materials.
