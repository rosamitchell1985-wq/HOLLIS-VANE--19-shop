Frequently asked questions

Twelve questions that account for most of what reaches client care. If yours is not here, the contact form reaches four people who answer within one business day.

No. We do not run seasonal sales, Black Friday promotions, flash sales or private discount codes. The only reductions we ever apply are on genuinely faulty seconds and on ex-photography samples, both sold from the studio once a year and announced by email with the fault described. The reasoning is set out in Our Story.

Ground shipping within the United States is free on orders of $250 or more. Below that it is a flat $9 regardless of how many items are in the order. Two-day and overnight options are $18 and $35. International shipping to Canada, the United Kingdom and the EU is $35 flat, with duties and taxes payable by the recipient. Full detail on Shipping & Delivery.

Thirty days from the date of delivery. Exchanges are free both ways: we send you a prepaid label and ship the replacement at no charge. Refund returns carry a flat $7 label fee deducted from the credit, unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong thing, in which case we pay everything. Made-to-order pieces can be refunded but not exchanged. Full process on Returns & Exchanges.

XS to XXL, cut on a single block rather than split by gender, with US numeric equivalents listed in the Size Guide. Outerwear runs generous, so take the smaller size if you are between two. Knitwear runs true except the Oversized Merino Cardigan, which is a full size large by design. Every product page lists the model height and the size worn.

Four pieces are cut only after you order them: The Shearling Car Coat at nine weeks, The Weekend Holdall at six, The Leather Belt at three, and monogrammed card cases at two. Lead times are measured from payment to dispatch and we email at each stage. Because there is no second one on a shelf, made-to-order pieces can be refunded within 30 days but cannot be exchanged for a different size or specification.

Free for the life of the garment on anything we made, with no receipt and no time limit. That covers reknitting moth holes, reweaving snags in woven wool, rebuttoning, hemming and sleeve shortening. Leather restitching and zip replacement are charged at cost because the workshop is in Spain. In 2025 we repaired 214 garments, the oldest from 2017.

Because every additional colour is a dyehouse minimum of roughly 300 kg and a separate inventory line that eventually has to be discounted if it does not sell. Not discounting is the entire model, so we keep the palette narrow and put the money into cloth instead. It also means everything in the collection works with everything else, which is the point of a capsule.

Stocked pieces are reproduced continuously, so a sold-out size normally returns within four to six weeks. Limited capsule runs are produced once and are not repeated. If a size you want is gone, write to client care and we will tell you honestly whether it is coming back and roughly when, or add you to a restock note for that specific size.

Yes, two ways. Our studio at 214 Lafayette Street is open by appointment on Thursdays and Fridays, 11:00 to 17:00, with every size on the rail and no obligation to buy. Twelve US retailers and two abroad also carry a selection, listed with addresses on Stockists. Stockists carry part of the collection, not all of it.

Less often than you think. Air knitwear between wears rather than washing it. When it does need washing, use a wool cycle at 30 degrees Celsius or hand wash cool with cashmere shampoo, then dry flat and reshape while damp. Never hang wet knitwear. Expect light pilling on cashmere for the first few wears, then it settles. Full instructions are on each product page.
