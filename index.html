The house

We make one collection. It does not go out of season, and it does not go on sale.

Hollis & Vane began in 2016 with eleven pieces and a single rule: nothing enters the collection unless it replaces something the wearer already owns. Ten years later the capsule holds twenty-eight styles. Pieces leave it when a mill closes or a better version exists. Nothing leaves it because a season ended.

We sell directly, from our own studio on Lafayette Street, with no wholesale markup layered on top. That is why a double-face wool coat that would carry a four-figure price under a department store margin costs $1,290 here. You can read the full arithmetic in Our Story.

How the business works

No wholesale markup, no seasonal discounting, no theatre. Here is the whole model on one screen.

Direct to consumer, in USD

Every price on this site is the price you pay in United States dollars. We sell from our own studio and ship from our own warehouse in Secaucus, New Jersey. Sales tax is calculated at checkout for states where we have nexus.

A seasonless capsule plus dated drops

Twenty-eight styles stay in stock year round. Two or three times a year we add a limited run, produced once and not repeated. The next two are fixed:

  • Capsule 09, the winter outerwear run: Thursday 10 September 2026, 10:00 ET.
  • Capsule 10, cashmere and leather: Thursday 5 November 2026, 10:00 ET.

We do not run countdown clocks and we do not tell you how many units are left. When a drop sells through, the page says so.

Shipping and returns

Ground shipping within the United States is free on orders of $250 or more. Below that it is a flat $9. Returns and exchanges are open for 30 days from delivery. We pay return shipping on exchanges and on faulty goods; you pay a flat $7 label fee on a refund return, deducted from the credit.

Full detail: Shipping & Delivery and Returns & Exchanges.

Made to order and pre-order

Four pieces are cut only after you order them, which removes the guesswork from our production run and the deadstock from our warehouse. Lead times are stated honestly on each product page and currently run from four to nine weeks.

Gift cards and referrals

Digital gift cards never expire and carry no fees. Refer someone and you each receive $40 after their first order ships. Details on Gift Cards & Referrals.
