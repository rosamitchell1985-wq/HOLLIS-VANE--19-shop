Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 August 2026. Hollis & Vane Ltd., 214 Lafayette Street, Suite 4, New York, NY 10012, United States.

This policy explains what personal information Hollis & Vane Ltd. collects when you use www.hollisandvane.com, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website, to our email programme, and to orders placed with us directly. It does not cover the independent retailers listed on our Stockists page, who operate their own policies.

We are the data controller. You can reach us at privacy@hollisandvane.com, by telephone on +1 (212) 555-0148, or by post at the address above.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us.

  • Order information. Name, shipping address, billing address, email address, telephone number, the items ordered and any notes you add.
  • Payment information. Card details are entered directly into our payment processor and are never stored on our servers. We receive only the card brand, the last four digits, the authorisation result and the billing postcode.
  • Contact and enquiry information. Anything you type into the contact form, the wholesale enquiry form or an email to us, including an order number if you supply one.
  • Email subscription information. Your email address and the date and source of your consent.
  • Appointment and repair information. Your name, contact details and a description of the garment and the work requested.

Information collected automatically.

  • Essential technical data. IP address, browser type and version, device type, and the pages requested. This is written to server logs and is necessary to deliver the site and to defend it against abuse.
  • Analytics data, only with your consent. Pages viewed, time on page, referring site, approximate city-level location derived from IP, and whether you completed an order.
  • Advertising data, only with your consent. Identifiers set by advertising cookies that allow our advertising partners, including Google, to measure and personalise advertising.
  • Local storage. Your bag contents and your cookie choices are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are not transmitted to us and we cannot read them remotely.

We do not collect sensitive categories of personal information such as health data, biometric data, precise geolocation, racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, or trade union membership. We do not ask for them and we do not infer them.

2. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To take and fulfil orders, including payment, shipping, returns, exchanges and repairs. Basis: performance of a contract with you.
  • To answer enquiries sent through the contact form, by email or by telephone. Basis: performance of a contract, or our legitimate interest in responding to you.
  • To send drop and Journal notices to people who have asked for them. Basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To keep accounting and tax records. Basis: compliance with a legal obligation.
  • To secure the site against fraud, abuse and automated attack. Basis: our legitimate interest in protecting the service.
  • To measure how the site is used and to advertise it. Basis: your consent, given through the cookie banner.

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device. We group ours into three categories.

  • Essential. Required for the site to function: keeping your bag, remembering your cookie choices, and protecting checkout against fraud. These are set without consent because the site cannot work without them.
  • Analytics. Aggregate measurement of how pages and products perform. Set only after you accept.
  • Advertising. Set by our advertising partners to measure campaigns and to personalise advertising, including on Google properties and on sites in the Google Display Network. Set only after you accept.

Nothing in the analytics or advertising categories is set before you make a choice. The consent banner appears on your first visit with three options: accept all, reject non-essential, and manage preferences, which lets you enable one category and not the other. You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer of every page, which clears your stored choice and reopens the banner.

The full table of individual cookies, their purposes and their lifetimes is on our Cookie Policy page.

4. Third parties we share information with

We do not sell personal information for money. We share it only with the processors and partners needed to run the business, and only to the extent each of them needs.

  • Payment processing. Our payment processor receives your card details, name, billing address and order value in order to take payment and prevent fraud.
  • Shipping carriers. Your name, address, telephone number and email so that a parcel can be delivered and tracked.
  • Email service provider. Your email address and consent record, used only to send messages you have asked for.
  • Website hosting and security. Server logs including IP address.
  • Google. Where you have consented to analytics or advertising, Google acts as a service provider or, in some advertising contexts, as an independent controller. Google may use cookies and identifiers to measure this site's performance, to serve advertising on Google properties and on partner sites, and to measure whether that advertising worked. Google's own handling of this data is described in its privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Professional advisers and authorities. Accountants, auditors and lawyers where necessary, and law enforcement where we are legally required to respond.

If Hollis & Vane were ever sold or merged, customer information would transfer as part of the business, and we would tell affected customers by email before the transfer took effect.

5. Advertising choices, including Google

You can control advertising in several independent ways, and any of them works on its own.

  • Reject the advertising category in our cookie banner, or change it later through the Cookie Settings link in the footer.
  • Opt out of personalised Google advertising through Google Ad Settings at adssettings.google.com, which applies across Google services regardless of your choice here.
  • Opt out of participating vendors through the Digital Advertising Alliance at optout.aboutads.info or the Network Advertising Initiative at optout.networkadvertising.org.
  • Send a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser. We treat a GPC signal as a valid opt-out of sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising for California residents.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies will stop the bag and checkout from working.

6. How long we keep information

  • Order and transaction records: seven years, to satisfy United States federal and New York State tax record-keeping requirements.
  • Contact form and email correspondence: twenty-four months from the last message, then deleted.
  • Email subscription records: until you unsubscribe, plus twelve months to evidence that consent existed.
  • Repair records: for the life of the garment, because the service has no time limit and the history is useful when a piece comes back.
  • Server security logs: ninety days.
  • Analytics data: fourteen months, in aggregate form.
  • Wholesale enquiries: twenty-four months, whether or not the enquiry led to an account.

7. Children's privacy

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. We do not direct any part of this site or our advertising at children, and we do not sell products designed for children.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, write to privacy@hollisandvane.com and we will delete the information and any associated account promptly. Where the California minor provisions apply, users under 18 who are registered may request removal of content they have posted.

8. California residents: CCPA and CPRA

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act gives you specific rights.

Categories of personal information we have collected in the last twelve months: identifiers (name, email, postal address, telephone number, IP address); customer records (billing and shipping details, order history); commercial information (products purchased or considered); internet activity (pages viewed, referring site); and inferences drawn from that activity, but only where you consented to analytics or advertising.

Sources: directly from you, automatically from your device, and from our shipping and payment processors.

Business purposes: as described in section 2 above.

Sale and sharing. We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. However, when you consent to advertising cookies, the resulting disclosure to advertising partners may constitute "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under the CPRA. You may opt out of that at any time through our Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information page, through the Cookie Settings link in the footer of every page, or by sending a Global Privacy Control signal.

Your rights. You have the right to know what we collect and why; to access a copy of your personal information; to correct inaccurate information; to delete your information, subject to exceptions such as completing a transaction or meeting our tax record-keeping obligations; to opt out of sale or sharing; to limit the use of sensitive personal information, which in our case is not applicable because we do not collect any; and not to be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights. We do not offer financial incentives in exchange for personal information.

How to exercise them. Email privacy@hollisandvane.com with the subject line "California privacy request", or call +1 (212) 555-0148. We verify identity by matching the details you give us to an existing order or subscription record; we may ask for the order number and the last four digits of the payment card. We respond within 45 days and may extend once by a further 45 days, telling you if we do. An authorised agent may act for you with written permission.

9. Visitors from the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom

We are a United States business, but we ship to the UK and EU and this site is accessible there. If the General Data Protection Regulation or the UK GDPR applies to you, you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdrawal of consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Personal information you give us is processed in the United States. Where we transfer personal information out of the EEA or the UK, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, with the UK Addendum where relevant, together with supplementary technical measures including encryption in transit and at rest.

You have the right to complain to your national supervisory authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office. Please contact us first if you can, because most issues are quicker to resolve directly.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS with TLS. Access to order data is limited to the people who need it, protected by multi-factor authentication, and reviewed quarterly. Card details never touch our systems. No security measure is perfect, and if a breach affects your personal information we will notify you and the relevant regulators within the timeframes the law requires.

11. Links to other sites

Pages on this site link to third parties, including our stockists and the opt-out tools named above. We are not responsible for their content or their privacy practices, and this policy does not apply once you leave hollisandvane.com.

12. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top of the page. If the change materially affects how we use your personal information, we will tell subscribers by email and place a notice on the site for thirty days before it takes effect.

13. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: privacy@hollisandvane.com. Everything else: clientcare@hollisandvane.com or the contact form. By post: Hollis & Vane Ltd., Attn: Privacy, 214 Lafayette Street, Suite 4, New York, NY 10012, United States. By telephone: +1 (212) 555-0148.
