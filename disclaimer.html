This page sets out the limits of what you should rely on when reading this site, and states our position on advertising, affiliate links and sponsored content.

1. General information

Everything published here is written in good faith and reviewed before publication. It is general information about the products we make and the materials they are made from. It is not professional advice, and it is not a substitute for the specific guidance of a tailor, a leatherworker, a textile conservator or a lawyer.

2. Product descriptions, measurements and photography

Measurements are taken from production garments and stated in inches. Manufacturing tolerance on a knitted or hand-finished garment is real, typically up to half an inch on a body measurement, and a piece within that tolerance is not faulty. Model heights and the sizes worn are stated so you can judge fall, but a photograph of one person is not a prediction about how a garment will fit you.

Natural materials vary between lots and hides. Vegetable-tanned leather changes colour with use, undyed lambswool differs from fleece to fleece, and shearling is matched per coat. This variation is a property of the material rather than a defect, and it is not covered by our returns process for faulty goods, though our normal 30-day return right always applies.

3. Care guidance

Care instructions on this site and on our labels are our best guidance based on the specific construction of our own garments. Follow them at your discretion. We cannot accept responsibility for damage caused by washing, cleaning or altering a garment contrary to its label, or for the outcome of any third-party cleaner or tailor. If you are unsure, ask client care before proceeding, and if something has already gone wrong, ask anyway, because we can usually repair it.

4. Figures and claims

Where we publish numbers, including the cost breakdown on Our Story, the production and repair figures on Sustainability & Materials, and the wardrobe survey in the Journal, they are our own internal figures, unaudited, accurate to the best of our knowledge on the date of publication, and rounded. Costs change with exchange rates and volumes. The wardrobe survey covered eleven self-selected customers and is described in the article itself as anecdotal rather than as research.

5. External links

This site links to third parties, including our stockists, industry opt-out tools and standards bodies. We do not control those sites, we are not responsible for their content, accuracy or availability, and a link is not an endorsement of everything on the destination. Once you leave hollisandvane.com, their terms and privacy policies apply.

6. Affiliate disclosure

We do not participate in any affiliate programme and we earn no commission from any link on this site. No product recommendation here is paid for, and no external link generates revenue for us.

If that ever changes, we will do three things: label the affected link at the point of use, state the relationship in plain language on the page where it appears, and update this section. That is what the Federal Trade Commission's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising require, and it is what we would want as readers.

7. Advertising

Some pages carry advertising, clearly labelled "Advertisement" and visually separated from our own content. Advertising slots never appear on legal pages or the error page, never above the main content of a page, and never adjacent to navigation or buttons in a way that could cause an accidental click. Advertisers have no influence over our editorial content, our product descriptions or our Journal, and we do not accept advertising that imitates our own layout.

8. Testimonials and reviews

We do not publish customer reviews or star ratings on this site, and we do not solicit them. Where a customer is quoted in the Journal, they are a real named person who gave permission for that specific quote. We have never published a fabricated review and we do not pay for coverage or gift product in exchange for it.

9. Availability and errors

We try to keep the site accurate and available, but we do not warrant that it will be uninterrupted or free of errors. Typographical errors in price or description are corrected when found, and our approach to a mispriced order is set out in section 3 of the Terms of Service: we tell you and give you the choice, and we never simply charge more.

10. Contact

If anything on this site looks wrong, please tell us at clientcare@hollisandvane.com. Corrections are made on the page with a note describing what changed.
