Capsule 08  /  Released 12 February 2026

Five pieces joined the capsule

We add to the collection two or three times a year and we take things out at the same rate. These five arrived in February, replacing a cotton knit, a slim trouser and a shirt whose mill in Guimaraes stopped weaving the cloth we used.

The Wide-Leg Wool Trouser$340

What is coming, and when

Two drops are scheduled and both dates are fixed. We publish them as soon as production is confirmed, and if a date slips we say so on this page rather than quietly moving it.

  • Capsule 09, winter outerwear. Thursday 10 September 2026, 10:00 ET. A shorter version of The Mono Overcoat, a wool and cashmere scarf in a wider cut, and the return of the shearling in a second hide.
  • Capsule 10, cashmere and leather. Thursday 5 November 2026, 10:00 ET. Two knitwear weights and a smaller version of The Leather Tote sized for a 13 in laptop.

Limited runs are produced once. When a size sells through, the page says sold through and we do not take backorders on it. There is no countdown timer on this site and we do not publish unit counts, because both are usually theatre.

Get the note, not the newsletter

One email goes out the day before a capsule opens. If you have asked us to watch a specific size, you get a second email when it is back. That is the entire programme. No weekly sends, no discount codes, no list sharing.

Specifications

Fabric, fit, care and sizing for each of the five new pieces, with model height and the size worn.

The Poplin Shirt

$215

A straight shirt in a dense two-ply cotton poplin, cut with a soft collar that stands without fusing and a single patch pocket. It is the piece we changed most between Capsule 07 and 08: the body is now an inch wider through the waist and the sleeve half an inch longer.

Fabric

  • 100% organic cotton poplin, 2 ply, 120 g per square metre, GOTS certified
  • Woven in Guimaraes, Portugal
  • Soft collar and cuff, no fusible interlining
  • Mother of pearl buttons

Fit

  • Straight through the body, squared hem
  • Body length: 29 in on size M
  • Cut to be worn untucked or tucked
  • Sleeve finishes at the wrist bone

Care

  • Machine wash warm, tumble dry low
  • Iron the collar and cuffs while slightly damp
  • The poplin softens noticeably after five washes
  • Collar and cuff replacement at our studio, at cost

Sizing

  • XS to XXL, equivalent to US 0 through US 18
  • Model is 5 ft 10 in and wears a size S
  • Take your usual size
  • Measurements in inches in the Size Guide

The Wide-Leg Wool Trouser

$340

A flat-front trouser in the same Huddersfield wool suiting as the blazer, cut wide from the hip and finished with a 1.5 inch turn-up. The waistband is curtained and the seat has 2 inches of let-out, so a tailor can adjust it properly.

Fabric

  • 100% virgin wool tropical suiting, 310 g per square metre
  • Milled in Huddersfield, England
  • Curtained waistband, Italian pocketing in cotton
  • Horn hook and bar closure

Fit

  • Sits at the natural waist
  • Wide and straight from the hip, 9.5 in leg opening on size M
  • Inseam 30 in as supplied, with 2 in of hem allowance
  • Free hemming to your length before shipping, on request

Care

  • Dry clean, or hand wash cold and dry flat if you accept a softer finish
  • Press with a cloth to avoid shine on the wool
  • Hang from the cuff on trouser clamps
  • Free hem alterations for life

Sizing

  • XS to XL, equivalent to US 0 through US 14
  • Model is 5 ft 8 in and wears a size S
  • If between sizes, take the larger and have the waist taken in
  • Measurements in inches in the Size Guide

The Straight Denim, Raw

$250

A raw 13.5 oz selvedge denim from Okayama, cut straight from the knee with a mid rise. Unwashed and unsanforized by choice, which means it will shrink about an inch in the leg the first time it is washed and then stop.

Fabric

  • 13.5 oz raw selvedge denim, 100% cotton
  • Woven on shuttle looms in Okayama, Japan
  • Sewn in Los Angeles, California
  • Copper rivets, no bar tacks at the pocket mouth

Fit

  • Mid rise, straight from the knee
  • Leg opening 7.5 in on size M
  • Inseam 32 in as supplied
  • Expect roughly 1 in of shrinkage in length on first wash

Care

  • Wear for at least two months before the first wash if you want visible fades
  • Machine wash cold inside out, hang dry
  • Never dry clean raw denim
  • Free repairs to the crotch and knees for life, in matching thread

Sizing

  • XS to XXL, equivalent to US 24 through US 36 waist
  • Model is 6 ft 0 in and wears a size M
  • Buy the waist you measure; raw denim relaxes about half an inch with wear
  • Waist conversion table in the Size Guide

The Silk Slip Dress

$395

Cut on the bias from a 19 momme sand-washed silk, which is heavy enough to fall rather than cling. Adjustable straps, French seams throughout, and a hem that finishes below the knee.

Fabric

  • 100% mulberry silk, 19 momme, sand washed
  • Woven and dyed in Hangzhou, China at an OEKO-TEX certified mill
  • French seams throughout, no exposed overlocking
  • Adjustable straps with a 2 in range

Fit

  • Bias cut, skims rather than clings
  • Length: 44 in from shoulder on size M
  • Hem finishes below the knee on a 5 ft 8 in frame
  • Bust is cut with ease; the bias does the shaping

Care

  • Hand wash cool with silk detergent, or dry clean
  • Dry flat in shade; sunlight will yellow silk over time
  • Cool iron on the reverse while slightly damp
  • Strap and seam repairs at our studio, at cost

Sizing

  • XS to XL, equivalent to US 0 through US 14
  • Model is 5 ft 11 in and wears a size XS
  • Bias garments run long; we shorten free of charge on request
  • Measurements in inches in the Size Guide

The Column Knit Dress

$445

A straight knitted dress in the same 12 gauge merino as the polo knit, with a boat neck and a small side vent at the hem. It travels folded and comes out of a bag without a crease.

Fabric

  • 100% extra-fine merino wool, 12 gauge
  • Responsible Wool Standard certified, mulesing free
  • Knitted in Dumfries, Scotland
  • Fully fashioned panels, side vents 4 in

Fit

  • Straight column with no waist shaping
  • Length: 46 in from shoulder on size M
  • Boat neck sits at the outer collarbone
  • Long sleeve finishing past the wrist

Care

  • Machine wash on a wool cycle at 30 degrees Celsius
  • Dry flat and reshape while damp
  • Store folded
  • Free reknitting of holes and snags, for life

Sizing

  • XS to L, equivalent to US 0 through US 12
  • Model is 5 ft 9 in and wears a size S
  • Take your usual size; the knit accommodates a range

What left the collection

The Cotton Rib Knit, The Tapered Trouser and the previous poplin shirt came out in February. The knit was replaced by a better version of the same idea. The trouser was cut too close to work over boots, which customers told us for two years. The shirt lost its cloth when the mill retired the loom.

If you own any of the three and need a repair, we still hold the patterns and the thread. Write to client care.

Pre-order and made to order

Four pieces in the collection are cut after you order. The Shearling Car Coat takes nine weeks, The Weekend Holdall six, The Leather Belt three, and monogrammed card cases two. Those lead times are measured from payment to dispatch and we email at each stage.

Made to order pieces can be returned for a refund within 30 days but cannot be exchanged for a different size or specification, because there is no second one waiting on a shelf. Full terms on Returns & Exchanges.
