California privacy

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Last updated 17 August 2026. Hollis & Vane Ltd., 214 Lafayette Street, Suite 4, New York, NY 10012, United States.

We do not sell personal information for money and never have. However, if you consent to advertising cookies on this site, the resulting disclosure of identifiers to advertising partners may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under the California Privacy Rights Act. This page is how you stop that.

The fastest way

Use the Cookie Settings link in the footer of any page, then choose Reject non-essential, or open Manage preferences and turn the advertising category off. That takes effect immediately in this browser and requires no verification, no account and no email address.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a valid opt-out request automatically and you do not need to do anything else on this page.

Opting out everywhere, not just this browser

A cookie-level opt-out applies to the browser you are using. To apply the request to your customer record across all devices, send us a request and we will apply it to any email address and order history we hold for you.

  • Email privacy@hollisandvane.com with the subject line "Do not sell or share".
  • Call +1 (212) 555-0148, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 ET.
  • Write to Hollis & Vane Ltd., Attn: Privacy, 214 Lafayette Street, Suite 4, New York, NY 10012, United States.
  • Use the contact form and choose "Privacy or data request" as the subject.

Include the email address you use with us. You do not need to give a reason.

Verification

An opt-out of sale or sharing does not require identity verification and we will not ask you for more information than we need to route it. For requests to access, correct or delete personal information, we do verify identity, by matching the details you provide against an existing order or subscription record. We may ask for the order number and the last four digits of the payment card used. If we cannot verify you, we will tell you why and what would help.

Authorised agents

You may use an authorised agent. We will ask for written permission signed by you, and we may contact you directly to confirm. An agent acting under a valid power of attorney does not need separate written permission.

Timing

Opt-out requests are actioned within 15 business days as required by the CPRA, and in practice within two. Access, correction and deletion requests are answered within 45 days, extendable once by a further 45 days if the request is complex, in which case we tell you before the first period ends.

Your other California rights

You also have the right to know what personal information we collect and why, to receive a copy of it, to correct inaccuracies, to delete it subject to legal exceptions such as our seven-year tax record obligation, and not to be treated differently for exercising any of these rights. We do not offer financial incentives for personal information, so there is no incentive programme to withdraw from. Full detail, including the categories of information we collect and who receives it, is in section 8 of our Privacy Policy.

If we say no

If we decline a request, in whole or in part, we tell you which exception applies and why. If you disagree, write back and a second person reviews it. You may also complain to the California Privacy Protection Agency or the California Attorney General.

Residents of other states

Residents of Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon and other states with comprehensive privacy laws have broadly equivalent rights, including the right to opt out of targeted advertising. Use the same routes above and we will apply the correct state framework to your request. We do not restrict these controls to California residents in practice: if you ask us to stop sharing your information for advertising, we stop, wherever you live.
