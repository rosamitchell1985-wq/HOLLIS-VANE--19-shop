Fit

Size guide

All measurements are in inches and describe the garment, not the body, unless stated otherwise. We cut on a single block from XS to XXL rather than splitting the collection by gender, so read the numbers rather than the letter.

How to measure yourself

You need a soft tape and a garment you already own that fits the way you want ours to fit. Measuring the garment flat is more reliable than measuring your body, because it accounts for the ease you actually like.

  • Chest. Lay the garment flat, buttoned or closed. Measure across one inch below the armhole, then double it.
  • Waist. Measure across the narrowest point of the garment and double it. For trousers, measure the waistband end to end and double it.
  • Shoulder. Measure from shoulder seam to shoulder seam across the back.
  • Sleeve. From the centre back neck, across the shoulder, down to the cuff edge.
  • Length. From the base of the collar seam at centre back, straight down to the hem.

How our sizes behave

  • Outerwear runs generous. Between sizes, take the smaller one.
  • Knitwear runs true, except the Oversized Merino Cardigan, which is a full size large by design.
  • Shirting runs true with a straight body and no waist suppression.
  • Raw denim shrinks about one inch in length on the first wash and relaxes about half an inch in the waist with wear.
  • Bias-cut silk runs long. We shorten it free of charge before shipping if you ask.

If you are between sizes and cannot decide, write to client care with the measurements of a garment you already own. We answer with a recommendation and the flat measurements of both candidate sizes.

If the size is wrong

Exchanges are free both ways within 30 days of delivery, including the label we send you and the shipment of the replacement. We hold the exchange stock for seven days from the moment you start the exchange, so nothing sells out underneath you.

Made-to-order pieces are the exception. They can be refunded but not exchanged for a different size, because there is no second one on a shelf. Read the detail on Returns & Exchanges.

Alterations we do for free

  • Shortening sleeves on The Long Trench and The Mono Overcoat.
  • Hemming The Wide-Leg Wool Trouser to your inseam before it ships.
  • Shortening the hem on The Silk Slip Dress.
  • Re-punching a belt after a change in waist size.

Ask at checkout in the order notes, or write to client care within an hour of ordering. Altered pieces remain returnable for a refund.
