Sizing, honestly

Our garments are cut generously through the shoulder and body. If you are between sizes on outerwear, take the smaller one: the drop shoulder already adds room. On knitwear, take your usual size unless you want the sleeve to cover the hand.

Every product page lists the model height and the size worn, so you can judge the fall against your own frame. Full measurements in inches are in the Size Guide.

What a purchase includes

  • Free ground shipping in the United States on orders of $250 or more, flat $9 below that.
  • 30 days to return or exchange from the date of delivery.
  • Free return shipping on exchanges and on anything faulty.
  • Repairs at cost for the life of the garment, including reweaving and releathering.
