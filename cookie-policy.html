Cookie Policy

Last updated 17 August 2026. Hollis & Vane Ltd., 214 Lafayette Street, Suite 4, New York, NY 10012, United States.

This policy lists what this site stores on your device, why, and for how long. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy, which covers personal information more broadly.

1. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file a site asks your browser to store. Related technologies include local storage, which keeps data in your browser without sending it back to the server on every request, and pixels, which are tiny image requests used for measurement. We use cookies, local storage and, only if you consent to advertising, third-party pixels.

2. Consent, and what happens before you choose

On your first visit a banner appears at the bottom of the screen with three choices: accept all, reject non-essential, and manage preferences, which lets you turn analytics and advertising on or off separately. The banner does not block the page and you can read the site while it is open.

Until you choose, only essential storage is used. No analytics and no advertising cookies are set. Rejecting has no effect on the shop: the bag, checkout, returns and every page work identically either way.

Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage under the key hv_consent_v1. Because it is stored per browser and per device, you may see the banner again on a different device or after clearing your browser data.

3. Changing your mind

Use the Cookie Settings link in the footer of every page. It clears the stored choice and reopens the banner so you can choose again. You can also delete cookies and local storage directly in your browser settings, and you can use your browser's Global Privacy Control setting, which we honour as an opt-out of sharing for advertising purposes.

5. Google in particular

If you consent to advertising, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve advertising based on your prior visits to this site or other sites, and to measure whether that advertising worked. You can opt out of personalised Google advertising at any time at adssettings.google.com, independently of the choice you make here. Google describes its use of data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We also participate in industry opt-out tools: the Digital Advertising Alliance at optout.aboutads.info and the Network Advertising Initiative at optout.networkadvertising.org. California residents can additionally use our Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information page.

6. Advertising on this site

Some pages carry clearly labelled advertising slots, marked "Advertisement" and visually separated from our own content. Advertising never appears on legal pages, on the error page, or above the main content of any page. We do not accept advertising that imitates our own editorial layout.

7. Do Not Track

There is no agreed industry standard for how sites should respond to the browser Do Not Track header, so we do not act on it. We do act on Global Privacy Control signals, which are standardised and legally recognised in California.

8. Questions

Write to privacy@hollisandvane.com or use the contact form and choose "Privacy or data request".
