Shipping and delivery

Everything ships from our own warehouse in Secaucus, New Jersey. Orders placed before 14:00 ET on a business day leave the same day. There is no handling fee, no fuel surcharge and no signature requirement unless you ask for one.

Dispatch times

  • Stocked pieces. Dispatched the same business day if ordered before 14:00 ET, otherwise the next business day.
  • Free alterations. Hemming, sleeve shortening or belt punching adds two to four business days before dispatch.
  • Made to order. The Shearling Car Coat is nine weeks, The Weekend Holdall six weeks, The Leather Belt three weeks and a monogrammed card case two weeks, all measured from payment to dispatch. We email at cutting, at completion and at dispatch.
  • Pre-order from a capsule drop. The dispatch date is stated on the product page before you pay and does not move without an email explaining why.

Where we ship

All fifty US states, Washington DC, US territories, APO, FPO and DPO addresses, Canada, the United Kingdom and the European Union. We do not currently ship anywhere else, because we cannot guarantee the return route.

We cannot deliver to PO boxes on two-day or overnight services, since the carriers will not accept them. Ground shipping to a PO box is fine.

Duties, taxes and customs

US orders are shipped inclusive of any applicable sales tax, which is calculated at checkout for states where we have nexus and shown before you pay. Nothing further is collected on delivery.

Orders to Canada, the UK and the EU are shipped delivery duty unpaid. The carrier will collect import duty, VAT or GST from the recipient before delivery, and the amount is set by the destination country rather than by us. As a rough guide, expect 20% VAT plus a carrier handling fee in the UK and EU, and GST plus provincial tax in Canada.

If you refuse a parcel rather than paying the duty, it returns to us and we refund the goods but not the outbound shipping or the return freight the carrier charges us.

Tracking

A tracking number is emailed the moment a parcel is scanned by the carrier. That link is always more current than we are, because it comes from the carrier directly. If tracking has not moved in three business days, write to client care and we will open a trace.

When something goes wrong

Check with neighbours and with your building first, since carriers frequently mark a parcel delivered when it has been left with a doorman or in a mail room. If it has genuinely not arrived after 24 hours, write to client care with your order number. We open a carrier trace, which takes up to eight business days, and if the parcel is not found we reship or refund in full at your choice. We do not ask customers to file police reports.

Photograph the outer box and the contents before unpacking further, then send the photographs to client care. We ship a replacement immediately from stock rather than waiting for the damaged one to come back, and we send a prepaid label for the return. If the piece was made to order, we recut it and tell you the new date the same day.

Write to client care immediately. If the order has not been picked we can change it in seconds. If it has already left, we can request an intercept with the carrier, which succeeds perhaps half the time and costs us $14, which we do not pass on. If it fails and the parcel is delivered to the wrong address, we treat it as a lost parcel and reship or refund.
