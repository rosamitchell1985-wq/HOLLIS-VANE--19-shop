Why we write these

Almost everything published about clothing manufacture is either a press release or a takedown. The middle ground, which is how a specific garment is actually put together and what that means for the person wearing it, mostly does not exist in public.

We write these slowly, three or four a year, and we publish the numbers we can support and the gaps we cannot close. If we get something wrong, write to client care and we will correct it on the page with a note saying what changed.

Get told when there is a new one

Journal pieces go out on the same list as capsule notices. That is one email before a drop and one when something is published here, which works out at six or seven emails a year.
