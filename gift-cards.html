Gifting

Gift cards and referrals

Two straightforward programmes with terms short enough to read. Gift cards never expire and carry no fees. Referrals pay both people, once, after the first order actually ships.

Digital gift cards

Available in $50, $100, $250, $500 and $1,000. Delivered by email within a minute of purchase, or scheduled to arrive on a date you choose. They can be sent to the recipient directly or to yourself if you would rather hand it over in person.

  • No expiry date, ever. New York law prohibits expiry on gift certificates and we would not use one regardless.
  • No dormancy fees, activation fees or service charges of any kind.
  • Redeemable against anything on the site, including made-to-order pieces.
  • Balances are partial-use: spend $180 of a $250 card and the remaining $70 stays on it.
  • If an order paid with a gift card is refunded, the money returns to the gift card balance, not to a bank account.
  • Gift cards themselves cannot be refunded or exchanged for cash, except where state law requires it.

To buy one, call +1 (212) 555-0148 or write to clientcare@hollisandvane.com and we will send a payment link. Card purchasing is being added to checkout in autumn 2026.

The referral program

Existing customers get a personal referral link from the account page or by asking client care. When someone new orders through it, you each receive $40 in store credit after their order ships and their 30-day return window closes.

  • Both people receive $40. The credit is not a percentage and does not scale with order value.
  • Credit is issued after the return window closes, which is 30 days after delivery. This is why the payout is not instant.
  • Store credit never expires and can be combined with a gift card.
  • You can refer as many people as you like. There is no cap and no tiered status.
  • Referrals cannot be made to yourself, to another account at your own address, or through paid advertising on our brand name.
  • If a referred order is refunded, the credit is reversed. If it is exchanged, the credit stands.

What we do not do

We do not run an affiliate program and we do not pay commission to publishers or creators. Nothing on this site is an affiliate link, and no page carries paid product placement. If that ever changes, every affected link will be marked at the point of use and disclosed under the FTC endorsement guidelines, and the change will be described on our Disclaimer page.

We do not gift product to creators in exchange for coverage. We lend samples to press and we ask for them back.

Corporate and bulk gifting

For orders of ten pieces or more, or for gift cards issued against a single invoice, write to clientcare@hollisandvane.com. We can arrange net-30 invoicing, a consolidated shipment and blind delivery to individual addresses. There is no bulk discount, for the reason set out in Our Story.
