What gauge actually tells you

Gauge is the number of needles per inch on the knitting machine. A 12 gauge merino polo is fine and smooth. A 3 gauge fisherman knit is thick and sculptural. Neither is better; they solve different rooms.

What matters more is ply and fibre length. Two-ply cashmere with a 36 mm staple pills far less than a single-ply yarn spun from short fibre, which is what most cashmere under $150 is made from. We wrote about how to tell the difference in the Journal.
