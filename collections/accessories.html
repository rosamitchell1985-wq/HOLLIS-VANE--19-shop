Vegetable tanning, and why it marks

Our leather is tanned with tree bark rather than chromium salts. It takes a Tuscan tannery around forty days instead of two, and the result is a hide that darkens with sun and hand oils rather than staying flat and uniform.

The trade is honest: vegetable-tanned leather spots if it gets soaked, and the first scratch will show. Six months in, the bag looks like yours and nobody else has one that matches. If that trade does not appeal, our canvas-lined structured bag is the more forgiving choice.
